NORTHSTAR COLD CHAIN
Incident report: stale temperature readings | Fictional training document
Incident date: 2026-11-12 | Reported: 2026-11-13

INCIDENT SUMMARY

On November 12, 2026, the dashboard showed stale temperature readings for 18
FrostBox-80 lockers at Northstar’s Nagpur demonstration depot after the Sentinel
monitoring application updated to version 2.8. The issue began at 09:12 and was
resolved at 12:30, for a total duration of 3 hours and 18 minutes.

No retailer inventory was affected. The depot was a training and demonstration
site, not a live retailer location. Staff checked the local temperature
displays during the incident. Those displays remained available, and logged
temperatures stayed within the demonstration range.

CAUSE

The version 2.8 reconnect handler could refresh the connection status without
refreshing the displayed measurement. The dashboard therefore appeared
connected while continuing to show an older reading. The refrigeration units
continued to operate normally; the fault affected the displayed monitoring
data, not the cooling system.

RESPONSE

Dev Mehta paused the demonstration and asked depot staff to compare each local
display with the dashboard. Leena Rao approved a rollback to Sentinel software
version 2.7. The dashboard readings refreshed after the rollback. The team
verified all 18 lockers before reopening the demonstration at 12:30.

FOLLOW-UP ACTIONS

Leena Rao owns the reconnect test for the proposed version 2.9. The test must
confirm that a fresh measurement is displayed after a connection is restored.
Dev Mehta owns the depot checklist requiring a local-display check during
future dashboard outages. Kiran Shah will use the same explanation in the
customer-support playbook if a retailer reports stale readings.

The incident report does not record a monetary loss because no customer
inventory was involved and no product was discarded.
