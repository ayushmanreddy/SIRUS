NORTHSTAR COLD CHAIN
Company profile | Fictional training document | Updated 2026-10-01

Northstar Cold Chain (NCC) is a fictional Pune-based company that builds
connected refrigerated lockers for neighborhood food shops. Its purpose is to
help small retailers keep temperature-sensitive groceries in good condition
without installing a full walk-in cold room. All details in this document are
invented for a SIRUS demonstration.

PRODUCT

The FrostBox-80 is NCC’s modular refrigerated locker. It has an 80-litre usable
capacity and is intended for small shops. Each FrostBox-80 can be paired with a
Sentinel temperature monitor. The monitor sends readings to the store dashboard
every five minutes while connected. A local temperature display remains
available if the dashboard connection is interrupted.

TEAMS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Mira Sen is the chief operating officer and chairs the weekly operations review.
Dev Mehta leads operations and manages production scheduling and depot readiness.
Leena Rao leads quality assurance and approves release testing for firmware and
hardware changes. Kiran Shah leads customer support and owns retailer service
communications. Arjun Das manages finance approvals and monthly budget reports.

The operations team works from a small assembly site in Pune. A demonstration
depot in Nagpur is used for training and product trials. These are the only
locations used in the fictional Northstar scenario.

OPERATING PRINCIPLES

Northstar does not release a product change just because a lab test passed.
Quality assurance must record the test result, operations must confirm the
rollout plan, and customer support must have a clear response guide before a
change is enabled for retailers.

The company’s quarterly operating target is at least 95 percent on-time delivery.
Customer support aims to acknowledge ordinary service requests within four
business hours. The on-time delivery target is separate from the temperature
monitor’s five-minute reporting interval.
