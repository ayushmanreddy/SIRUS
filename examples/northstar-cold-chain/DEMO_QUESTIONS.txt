NORTHSTAR COLD CHAIN — DEMONSTRATION QUESTIONS

All questions below refer only to the fictional files in this pack. Upload the
five numbered company documents first. Ask across all documents unless a step
specifically asks you to select a file in the SIRUS library.

1. What does the FrostBox-80 do, and what is its capacity?
   Expected: It is a modular refrigerated locker for small shops with 80 litres
   of usable capacity.
   Source: 01_company_profile.txt

2. What was Northstar’s Q3 on-time delivery rate, and how did it compare with
   the target?
   Expected: 92.9 percent versus a 95 percent target.
   Source: 02_q3_operations_report.txt

3. Why were nine Q3 shipments late?
   Expected: Sentinel-T4 sensors from the fictional supplier Veda Components
   arrived eight days later than scheduled.
   Source: 02_q3_operations_report.txt

4. During the November 12 incident, how many lockers were affected, how long
   did the issue last, and was customer inventory affected?
   Expected: 18 lockers; 3 hours and 18 minutes; no retailer inventory was
   affected because this was a demonstration depot.
   Source: 03_incident_report_2026-11-12.txt

5. How much of the demonstration budget remained on November 20?
   Expected: 165,000 fictional rupees remained from a 480,000-rupee budget after
   315,000 rupees had been spent.
   Source: 04_decision_log_2026-11-20.txt

6. What must happen before version 2.9 can be rolled out more widely?
   Expected: A 50-unit pilot, a passing reconnect test, then seven consecutive
   days without a stale-reading event and a go/no-go review. Automatic updates
   remain disabled until approval.
   Source: 04_decision_log_2026-11-20.txt

7. When should support escalate a temperature concern immediately?
   Expected: When the local display is above 8 degrees Celsius for more than
   30 minutes.
   Source: 05_customer_support_playbook.txt

8. What is the street address of Northstar’s Pune office?
   Expected: The documents do not provide a street address. SIRUS should say it
   cannot find enough information rather than inventing one.
