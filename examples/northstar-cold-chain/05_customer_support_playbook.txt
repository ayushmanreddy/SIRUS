NORTHSTAR COLD CHAIN
Retailer support playbook | Fictional training document | Updated 2026-11-21

SERVICE COMMITMENT

Northstar aims to acknowledge ordinary retailer service requests within four
business hours. The acknowledgement should confirm the request was received
and explain who will follow up. It is not a promise that every repair will be
completed within four hours.

TEMPERATURE ALERT TRIAGE

1. Ask the retailer to read the local temperature display on the FrostBox-80.
2. Ask whether the dashboard reading is current or appears stale.
3. If the local display is normal but the dashboard is stale, record the time
   and check the monitor connection. Do not claim that the refrigeration unit
   has stopped cooling based on a stale dashboard reading alone.
4. If the local display shows a temperature above 8 degrees Celsius for more
   than 30 minutes, escalate the case to operations immediately and advise the
   retailer to follow its own food-safety handling rules.
5. Record the locker identifier, local reading, dashboard status, and actions
   taken in the service ticket.

WARRANTY

FrostBox-80 units have an 18-month limited warranty beginning on the delivery
date. The warranty covers manufacturing defects under normal use. Damage from
improper handling after delivery is reviewed case by case.

KNOWN SOFTWARE ISSUE

The November 12, 2026 incident affected the dashboard reconnect behavior in
Sentinel version 2.8. The cooling system was not affected. Northstar rolled
back the demonstration depot to version 2.7 and is testing version 2.9.
Support staff must not promise a public release date for version 2.9; the
decision log sets a pilot review, not a public launch.
