NORTHSTAR COLD CHAIN
Q3 2026 operations report | Fictional training document | Issued 2026-10-08

SUMMARY

During Q3 2026, Northstar Cold Chain shipped 126 FrostBox-80 lockers. 117
shipments arrived by their committed dates and 9 arrived late, giving an
on-time delivery rate of 92.9 percent. The quarterly target was 95 percent.
Three units were returned for inspection, a return rate of 2.4 percent of
shipments. No return was classified as a food-safety event.

The report covers July 1 through September 30, 2026. Figures are fictional and
were created solely to demonstrate document retrieval.

DELIVERY DELAYS

The nine late shipments were held up by a delayed delivery of Sentinel-T4
temperature sensors from the fictional supplier Veda Components. The shipment
was eight days later than the purchase order date. Operations used available
stock to complete the first scheduled customer orders, then revised delivery
dates for the remaining orders. The delay did not change the FrostBox-80
refrigeration design.

CORRECTIVE ACTIONS

Dev Mehta will maintain a two-week buffer for Sentinel-T4 sensors during the
next production cycle. Arjun Das will review the carrying cost at the November
monthly finance meeting. The buffer should be reduced if supplier delivery
reliability returns to the agreed schedule for two consecutive months.

QUALITY AND SERVICE

Leena Rao’s team inspected all three returned units. Two had damaged door
seals from handling in transit; one had a loose temperature-monitor connector.
All three units were repaired and returned to service. Kiran Shah updated the
packing checklist to include a seal inspection before dispatch.

DECISION NEEDED

The operations review asked whether the sensor buffer should become permanent.
No permanent inventory policy was approved in Q3. The temporary two-week buffer
remains in place until the November finance review.
